# Copyright and third-party exclusions

Original ECHO Unity source, procedural robot geometry, authored materials,
scene layouts, documentation and simulator records are distributed under
GPL-3.0-or-later, matching the official [echoai](https://github.com/navywakura/echoai)
repository. The matching license and existing author notices are in `LICENSE`.
Publication changes are listed in `Docs/PUBLICATION.md`.

This grant covers the project's own work. It does not relicense Unity, Unity
packages, Adventure Forge graphics, or third-party works visible in historical
captures. Unity and its packages retain their respective licenses; the package
manifests identify dependencies, and their binaries are not bundled here.

**Adventure Forge — Sci-fi Spaceship**, by Endless Adventures Inc., supplied
graphics used in the private historical station. Its PNG/FBX files, imported
metadata, ZIP/AFPack archives and pack README are excluded. Filenames and hashes
in `Docs/ASSET-INVENTORY.json` document the original local import; they do not
contain the artwork or grant redistribution rights. Capture attribution is
descriptive; no license to extract or redistribute depicted third-party artwork
is granted. See [asset attribution and local setup](Docs/ASSETS.md).

The public station uses original primitive placeholders. The navigation lab,
robot, sensor code and mission logic are included. ECHO-5 is imported from the
official echoai checkout and is not duplicated in this repository.
