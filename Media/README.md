# Recorded development screenshots

These are unretouched frames extracted from the owner’s Unity recordings of
11 October 2026. They show the editor as recorded, including its UI and any
warnings. They are illustrations of development sessions, not a new evaluation
or evidence that ECHO-5 has passed its release gates.

- `navigation-lab.png`: navigation recording at 60 seconds, first-person camera,
  observed map and decision panel.
- `station-overview.png`: station recording at 15 seconds, the original five-room scene.
- `obstacle-replanning.png`: navigation recording at 115 seconds, an obstacle
  introduced in the scene alongside the camera and remembered map.

[PROVENANCE.json](PROVENANCE.json) gives original recording filenames, SHA-256
hashes and frame timestamps. Full recordings and audio are retained locally.

The recorded scenes contain Adventure Forge Sci-fi Spaceship graphics. Those
third-party asset files are not part of this repository. Screenshots do not
extend the asset pack’s license. See [asset notices](../Docs/ASSETS.md).
